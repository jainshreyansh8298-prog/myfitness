Online Fertility Yoga

  • AED 70
Overview

Online Fertility Yoga is a gentle, therapeutic yoga program designed to support women who are trying to conceive—whether naturally or while managing conditions like PCOS, PCOD, endometriosis, or blocked fallopian tubes. These live, guided sessions combine mindful movement, breathwork, and stress-relief techniques to help balance hormones, improve reproductive health, and enhance emotional well-being.

This holistic approach provides a nurturing space for your body and mind—whether you’re just beginning your fertility journey or seeking support after challenges.

Each Session Includes:

  • Customized yoga sequences designed to improve reproductive function and hormone regulation
  • Guided breathing techniques to reduce stress and calm the nervous system
  • Live corrections and support to ensure safe and effective practice
  • Final relaxation phase to leave you feeling calm, grounded, and hopeful

Important Notes:

  • Sessions are designed for individual participation only and the duration is 1 hr per session
  • Extra charges apply if more than one person attends
  • Cancellation charges apply if canceled less than 24 hours before your session

 You an also combine with Holistic Wellness:

Consider pairing Fertility Yoga with additional wellness services such as:

  • Nutrition Consultations to optimize your fertility-friendly diet
  • Therapeutic Massages to relieve tension and support hormone flow

Who Should Join:

  • Women preparing for natural conception
  • Those diagnosed with PCOS, endometriosis, or other reproductive conditions
  • Anyone looking to balance their hormones and reduce fertility-related stress
